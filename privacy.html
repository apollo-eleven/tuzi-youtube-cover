隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

本工具只访问频道所有者本人授权的 YouTube 频道，唯一用途是为该频道自己的视频上传封面图片。工具不收集、不保存、不分享任何其他个人数据，也不向任何第三方提供数据。授权凭据只保存在所有者本人的电脑上，所有者可随时在 Google 账号的「第三方访问」中撤销授权。

This tool only accesses the YouTube channel that its owner has authorized, for the sole purpose of uploading thumbnail images for that channel's own videos. It does not collect, store, or share any other personal data, and does not provide data to any third party. Authorization credentials are stored only on the owner's own computer, and the owner can revoke access at any time in their Google Account's third-party access settings.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
